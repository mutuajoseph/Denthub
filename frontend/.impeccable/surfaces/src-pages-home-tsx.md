# Home (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a patient (phone or desktop) looking for a trustworthy dentist in their Country. Action: Find a Dentist (aqua); secondary: Shop. Proof: the product itself (a real-shaped dentist card, search facets, local insurers/currency), never invented stats or credentials. Constraints: copy and figures come from region config + Site CMS; stats band hidden until real; light only; Emergency one tap away.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dental network drawn as a drafting-studio schematic: care is shown routing from patient to dentist, not advertised. Refuses the category default of a photo of a smiling patient over a gradient with a stats band.

OWN-WORLD: White paper canvas, Ink #18181b type, Graphite #27272a tiles, Cloud #f4f4f5 sheets with 1px Steel hairlines, 16px cards, 12px compact buttons, Aqua #94faf0 only on the conversion button and selected markers, Lime #d4f796 only on the announcement strip; the Signal Sweep gradient (#07cddf to #9eed15) only on the one connector line, as DESIGN.md specifies for signal paths. Inter Tight display at -0.04em, Inter 500 UI, Chivo Mono for technical labels (county codes, insurer IDs, prices).

STORY: The visitor reads what DentHub does for them, sees a dentist match being routed from their search, understands it works in their Country, and presses Find a Dentist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: display headline (80px desktop / 44px phone), 1-line support copy in Slate, capability row (three facts with 12px graphite tiles), aqua Find a Dentist + graphite-outline Shop. Right (desktop): stacked Cloud/white sheets with hairline construction lines, a search-facet sheet (specialty, Subdivision, insurer) routed by a thin line to a floating Graphite dentist tile from the Site CMS hero card. Phone: the diagram collapses to the Graphite tile under the actions.

FORM: Pinned by the brief, not rolled: DESIGN.md "Layout" prescribes this asymmetric two-column hero with a layered diagram, followed by an Ink band and alternating text-led sections. No concept-seed run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
