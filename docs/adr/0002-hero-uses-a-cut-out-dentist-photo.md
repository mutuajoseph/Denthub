# The home hero uses one cut-out dentist photo

`docs/design/DESIGN.md` asks for contained product illustrations rather than
photography. The home hero is the one deliberate exception: a single
background-removed photo of a Black clinician (Unsplash, credited in
`frontend/src/assets/home/CREDITS.md`) stands on a drafting panel beside the
search sheet and match tile, because patients choosing a dentist respond to a
real person, not only a diagram. The cut-out keeps it inside the drafting-sheet
world (no full-bleed photography), identifying marks on the clothing are
painted out, and it sits next to fixture data labelled "Example". Everywhere
else, illustrations remain the default.
